# TNP Corp (The North Pole Corp)

The rival in the shipping game.  Discussed 2026-11-03.

## Known so far

- A corporate North Pole that runs on its own while the big guy sleeps.  The player fights the corporation itself.  **The big guy is never named.**
- TNP and our company each have a line on a stock ticker.  The second game's goal is keeping our line above theirs.
- TNP tries to steal shipments with tricks:
    - **Fake trucks** with tells: candy-cane mudflaps, a holly wreath on the grille, reindeer hoofprints, a plate reading N0RTH, sleigh bells on arrival.
    - **Elf spies** in borrowed gnome hats.  Gnomes are short; an elf comes up past the height chart on the dock wall.
    - **Fake paperwork:** stamps with snowflakes, holly, and candy canes; items not in the catalog; labels for the wrong region.
- Loading a fake truck hands the goods to TNP and pushes their ticker up.
- TNP's look: red and white candy stripes, holly green, snowflakes, sleigh-bell gold, a sterile corporate shine.  Our side is brass, oak, and soot.
