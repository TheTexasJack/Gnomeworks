# Alex's region write-ups (verbatim)

Alex's original text, received in two batches on 2026-11-15, kept word for word.  Both batches are merged into the gazetteer.  Her Notable Flora and Fauna are kept for later expansion.

```text
Gnomadica
----------------------
A mostly isolated archipelago with a thriving industry in making just about anything anybody could ask for. Monsters of all kinds make their home here but the major species here being the humble Gnome. With the unique biomes, flora and fauna can be found here that are no where else.
Of major interest to any explorer, the forests of Gnomadica do serve as a dumping ground for any excess creatures the factories manufacture so proceed with caution.
Notable Flora : Magmatic Algae, Invisible Tree, Improbable Cap
Notable Fauna : Vent Coral, Hyper-pod, Boom Whale
Gnomerica
----------------------
The central island of the archipelago with the most diverse biome spread. From the deserts of Gnafrica to the marshes of Slanic, there are plenty of places to explore. If the natural world is not your cup of tea then check out the heart of commerce Cogbottom. The beautiful bronze rooves shine in the sun complement the clean roads. They have recently got the pollution under control so now any tourist can breath easy while doing their shopping at a multitude of shops within Cogbottom.
Notable Flora : Moon Nut Tree, Shroom Lichen, Living Amber
Notable Fauna : Smog Ant, Barking Mimic, Salmon
Gnohio
----------------------
The centrel-west region of Gnomerica. Rolling plains scattered with farmland make up this region with a lone forest in its center. Farmers attempted to raze the forest long ago but were chased away by angry treants, now it is forbidden to enter the forest. This however does not stop the boldest of beings willing to brave angry treants. Should you wish to visit Gnohio, the rivers offer excellent fishing opportunity.
Notable Flora : Multi-eared Qurn, Ulmabeans, Treants
Notable Fauna : Moomoo, Oinkers, Bawkers
Gnafrica
----------------------
The southwest peninsula of Gnomerica. Thanks to the Dry Ridge the worst of the desertification is contained to this region. The environment as a whole is quite hostile with temperatures soaring to 82 C during the day and dipping to -5 C at night. Active volcanoes and roaming bandits are doing this region no favors either. Thankfully, the bandit problem is getting better thanks to the efforts of the gnomes of Cogbottom setting up guards along the major roads of Gnafrica.
Notable Flora : Obi Cactus, Rillo Succulent, Miragebloom
Notable Fauna : Fennec Hound, Dunehopper, False Korid
Slanic
----------------------
The southern region of Gnomerica. Delicate marshlands make up this region with almost fae like rules. Follow the rules and any explorer will have a pleasant experience. Break the rules and you will be lost in the fog. Because of these rules, there are few towns in this region. The only rules that seemed consistent was no intentionally destroying the marsh, no harassing the animals, and receiving a ridiculous name. After that the rules can change per person per visit seemingly at random.
Notable Flora : The Fog, Grilgrove Tree, Geist Cup
Notable Fauna : The Fog, Swamp Puppy, Regretful Sprite
Gno Dao Cradle
----------------------
The northeast peninsula of Gnomerica. Taken up majority by the Gnomish Heights mountain range makes traversal difficult. If one stays on level ground they can find the bay which is how this region got its namesake. Strange happenings occur frequently here with tears in the fabric of reality being common in the depths of the bay and the tops of the mountains. Should any explorer take to the depths of the mountains remember to visit Coghold beforehand for a handheld reality anchor lest you find yourself somewhere strange.
Notable Flora : Shifting Reeds, Impossible Grass, Crystalline Moss 
Notable Fauna : Cuttled Snail, Chocolate Goat, Living Pi
Gnufoundland
-----------------------
The northern island of the archipelago. From the Frozen Fields to the taiga to the snow caps, under the snow there is plenty to see in Gnufoundland. Cloudeer roam the fields if you're a hunter, lufzins love to roost in the cliffs if you fancy bird watching, Werewolf London has delicious warm beverages once the cold gets to you. Should you ever find yourself lost in the cold, there is always a cabin near.
Notable Flora : Ogre Breathe, Lobsterapple, Dognip
Notable Fauna : Cloudeer, Lufzins, Friendly Mimic
```
