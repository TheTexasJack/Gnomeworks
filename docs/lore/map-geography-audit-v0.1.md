# Gnomeworks map geography audit, v0.1

**Purpose:** Extract what the current gazetteer already says about terrain and relative placement, before drawing mountains, watersheds, biomes, roads, or settlement pins.  This is a research ledger, not a proposed finished map.

**Sources:** [Location Gazetteer v0.5](location-gazetteer-v0.13.md), its 100-place name pool in [place-names.md](place-names.md), and Nathan's hand-drawn master coastline.  Nathan has established that the main island is hundreds of miles across, approximately UK-sized.  The coastal outline is the geographic starting point; the draft's directional notes were largely invented before the map and are **not** canon.

## The two inventories

1. **Geographical elements:** Every already named natural feature or terrain-bearing site, plus physical features mentioned but not yet given a map label.
2. **Placement clues:** The currently written positions and neighboring relationships for **all 100** gazetteer entries.  These are evidence to test against the coastline and later terrain, not orders to force a placement.

**Confidence key:** **User constraint** = Nathan's main-island scale and supplied coastline.  **Draft clue** = proposed wording in the gazetteer.  **Open** = unresolved or potentially contradictory; do not force-fit it.

## Placement conflicts and decisions to keep open

| Issue | Working rule |
| --- | --- |
| The coastlines are the ground truth | The approved sketch comes before any invented directional assignment.  Do not trace named regions onto specific offshore shapes until Nathan identifies them. |
| The map is enormous | The main island is hundreds of miles across.  'Near', 'beside', and 'beyond' need an appropriate scale and could mean hours or days of travel. |
| West coast and north coast rivers | River's Belt is known in the draft to reach Port Gnaru, while North Ham'n'Gnome also claims a northern estuary.  These need not be the same waterway. |
| Eastern reefs cross a region boundary | Dungeon of the Deep is classed under Slaanilok but described below Port Gnasher's reef on the main-island coast. |
| Inner island versus outer island | Mycelia is a raised marsh island within Slanic.  It must not be confused with the large offshore islands on the master sketch. |
| Gnafrica geography is provisional | The draft calls Gnafrica a southwest peninsula, but there are offshore landmasses in the sketch.  Keep its physical outline undecided until the major region borders are drawn. |
| Temperature and rainfall not modeled | Northern snow, southern desert, foggy seas, hot springs, volcanoes, and wet southern marshes need latitude, elevation, prevailing winds, and watersheds checked before map placement. |
| Paths are not straight lines | Mountain roads, ferries, canals, and railway lines have only textual endpoints, not engineering alignments or travel durations. |
| Named POIs may not deserve master-map pins | The confetti museum, Fort Gnomehagen, historic institutions, shops, and single-object monuments should generally go on later regional or town maps. |
| Draft lore has authority limits | Claims involving wars, historical routes, or ancient devices are story proposals.  Use them as optional hints, never to override Nathan or Alex's geography choices. |
