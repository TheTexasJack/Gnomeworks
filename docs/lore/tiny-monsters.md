# Tiny Monsters (v0.2)

Tap a Tiny Monster on the shelf to open its entry, like a Pokedex.  11 families, 4 forms each (normal, glowing, goopy, evil).  The normal form comes out of the machine as a Rare; the other three only come from shinies.

## Open questions for the whole set

- Where do Tiny Monsters come from, and why does the machine make them?
- What makes a form glowing, goopy, or evil?

## Home regions and stamps (draft)

Each region's stamp shows the normal form of its home family.  Families also wander: a place can list Tiny Monsters from other regions in its Fauna line.

| Region | Home family | Stamp monster | Why it lives there |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Gnomerica | Owl | Hootgear | Nests in Woods of the Elder and sits on the Gnomish Farms scarecrows. |
| Gnohio | Mouse | Potamice | Mouse Reserve was made for them. |
| Gnafrica | Lizard | Magmander | Warm volcano rock, Auntie Ember, and Toast at the bakery. |
| Slanic | Slime | Jellybub | Gloopenstein and Slimes Akummin. |
| Gno Dao Cradle | Golem | Pebblem | Crystals on the Flats, Glowrock Retreat, and Big ole' Hole. |
| Gnufoundland | Fluffbat | Fluffbat | Glowbats sleep in the Frozen Fields barns. |
| Slaanilok | Turtle | Shroomtle | The islands already have turtle keepers. |
| Gni | Sprout | Bloomlet | The knights prize their shrubberies. |
| Shen Shin | Rabbit | Tickhop | Snow hares in the pines, and they kick shins. |
| Gnonmoon | Fox | Zapfox | Hopped the strait from Gnafrica; Lumifox glows under the big moon. |

Snake is the eleventh family and has no stamp.  It lives in the Gno Dao Cradle beside the Golems, around the Ancient Device and the 6th Age Ruins.
