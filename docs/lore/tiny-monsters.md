# Tiny Monsters

Tap a Tiny Monster on the shelf to open its entry, like a Pokedex.  11 families, 4 forms each (normal, glowing, goopy, evil).  The normal form comes out of the machine as a Rare; the other three only come from shinies.

## Open questions for the whole set

- Where do Tiny Monsters come from, and why does the machine make them?
- What makes a form glowing, goopy, or evil?
- Which region does each family live in?

## Entry template

```
### <Name> (<family>, <form>)

- Region:
- Lore tab text: a few sentences.
```
