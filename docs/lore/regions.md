# Regions

The places the shipping dock sends stock.  Waiting on Nathan and Mara's maps.

## What the game needs from each region

- **Name**, and why it's called that.
- **One color and one symbol**, so its stamp and label can be told apart at a glance with no reading.
- **Style notes:** what it looks like and who lives there.
- **A picture** for the Gnomopedia.
- **Lore:** a few sentences for the Gnomopedia.
- **Which Tiny Monsters live there** (feeds the Tiny Monster entries).
- **Who orders from there**, and what they want (feeds the order board).

Five to eight regions is a good range for kids.

## Region stamps (Nathan, 2026-11-15)

Stamps carry no names.  Each region's stamp is one color and one simple symbol, so a player who can't read yet can still match them.  The colors are the same as the map's region washes, so the packing room's wall map teaches the stamps.  Sheet: [stamps/region-stamps-v0.1.png](stamps/region-stamps-v0.1.png), drawn by `tools/stamps/make_stamps.py` as placeholders for Mara to repaint.

| Gnonmoon | Night blue `#4f5f86` | Crescent moon |

- No Christmas shapes on real stamps: snowflakes, holly, and the like are the TNP fake tells.
- Region stamps are separate from the complication stickers.  A Gni package gets the Gni helm stamp **and** a Shrubbery or Red Herring sticker.
- Tiny Monsters are not on stamps (dropped 2026-11-15).
