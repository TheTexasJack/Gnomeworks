# Regions

The places the shipping dock sends stock.  Waiting on Nathan and Mara's maps.

## What the game needs from each region

- **Name**, and why it's called that.
- **One color and one symbol**, so its stamp and label can be told apart at a glance with no reading.
- **Style notes:** what it looks like and who lives there.
- **A picture** for the Gnomopedia.
- **Lore:** a few sentences for the Gnomopedia.
- **Which Tiny Monsters live there** (feeds the Tiny Monster entries).
- **Who orders from there**, and what they want (feeds the order board).

Five to eight regions is a good range for kids.
