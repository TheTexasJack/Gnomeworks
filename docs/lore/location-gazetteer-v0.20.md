# Gnomeworks: Location Gazetteer (v0.20 Working Draft)


## How to read this draft

- **Location** means a *proposed* place on the one master map, described in relative directions so it can be moved when the map is drawn.  There are no fixed coordinates yet.
- **Region** gives the proposed parent area.  Gnomadica covers the mapped island and nearby offshore areas.  The North Pole and the wider world stay off the map.
- **Trade fields** appear only on places that buy our goods or make their own: cities and ports, villages, shops and attractions, and working mines or strongholds that produce, such as The Crafter's Mines (tools and food in, ore out) and Ogre's Hold.  Natural landmarks and roadside POIs never carry trade fields.  Broad regions, unworked landscapes, and abandoned ruins have none either.  Regional trade is represented through its individual producing and receiving locations.
- **Humanoid monsters are people.**  Ogres, goblins, giants, and their neighbors trade, work, and keep homes like anyone else.
- **Factory Imports / Factory Exports** list only goods that exist in the current Gnomeworks collectible catalog (`docs/INVENTORY.md` and `js/config.js`).  All items use their exact written names without emoji icons or tags.  A trade listing does not mean a shipping order is implemented.
- **Non-Factory Imports / Non-Factory Exports** list tangible, shippable goods outside the existing factory catalog, including fantasy objects.  Services, rumors, and activities are excluded.  These goods do not become new factory collectibles.
- **None** means no trade item of that type has been proposed yet; the place may still trade it later.
- **Flora** lists nearby plants.  **Fauna** lists nearby Tiny Monsters.  These habitats are suggestions for Nathan and Alex to approve.
- **Lore in v0.4** adds draft community histories, characters, connections, and fantasy traditions.  Humanoid species are people with ordinary jobs and different personalities; ancestry or appearance never determines someone's morality.
- This is a place registry for future shipping, lore cards, and expeditions.  A place can serve several purposes even though it is filed under one primary category here.  Some entries may remain hidden from players until discovered.
- **Bounties** is intentionally empty in every entry for future use.  These are story hooks only, with no quests or rewards built yet.
- The factory company name, machine origin, and regional histories remain open.  This file is a working draft published on GitHub for Nathan and Alex to review.  Publication does not establish canon.

## I. Major regions and the mapped area

### Gnomadica

- **Name:** Gnomadica
- **Location:** The whole chart: Gnome Island and the nearby isles, seas, and coast.
- **Region:** Greater mapped area (parent of all regions).
- **About:** The busy little corner of the world where somebody always needs a sock.  From the brass-smoked chimneys of Cogbottom to the sea lanterns of Slaanilok, every district has a reason to send a parcel and a different reason to refuse one.  Gnomes, fairies, giants, lamia, and other neighbors share roads, ferries, arguments, and recipes; the customs office has yet to devise a form big enough for a giant's fingerprint.
- **Major Facts:** Gnomadica is the ring of islands around a central mainland, and the Factory headquarters sits in Cogbottom.  The world beyond is vast and mysterious.  Sailors tell of friendly sea monsters that help ships across the ocean and merfolk who rescue crews caught in storms, and far-away lands tell of even stranger affairs.  The island-wide Postmaster's Bell rings whenever a packet travels outward from the Gnohio riverboats.  The first successful shipment was a single left sock, which the recipient immediately mailed back because it didn't match.  Old maps from different peoples give the same coastline different names, so surveyors trust the oldest landmarks over any one cartographer.
- **Flora:** Oak, river reeds, saltgrass, snowbells
- **Fauna:** Tickhop, Hootgear, Magmander, Shroomtle, Zapfox, Potamice, Fluffbat, Pebblem, Bloomlet, Cogsnake, Jellybub
- **Bounties:**

### Gnomerica

- **Name:** Gnomerica
- **Location:** Central Gnome Island, around the factory and main crossroads.
- **Region:** Gnomadica, central industrial region.
- **About:** Brass roofs, gear towers, and shouting shift foremen make this the busiest region, where odd ideas become even odder products.  Its towns are stitched together by the Central Rail, the Brass Road, and a surprising number of workers who swear their commute is downhill both ways.  Lamia engineers coil around broken steam pipes to reach awkward valves, while goblin bookkeepers keep honest ledgers and dishonest lunch schedules.
- **Major Facts:** Cogbottom is its manufacturing heart.  Its foundries supply Cogsworth and Pulley Junction, while Gnomish Farms feeds the morning shift.  The old public clock has shown lunch time for years, and everyone agrees it is polite to obey.  The Great Lunch Whistle once blew for nine hours because a pebble jammed the valve; the workers voted to call it a public holiday.  The Council of Standard Cogs has argued over the proper tooth count for a hundred years and still uses three different standards on the same railway.
- **Flora:** Brassleaf oak, gearvine
- **Fauna:** Hootgear, Cogsnake, Pebblem
- **Bounties:**

### Gnohio

- **Name:** Gnohio
- **Location:** West-central Gnome Island, along the broad rivers and rich farm country.
- **Region:** Gnomadica, western river region.
- **About:** Wide rivers, farms, and small bridges tie the western towns together, and locals claim every road leads downhill to a sandwich shop.  The region is named for General José Gnomanion Gnohio, a gnomish hero of the Republic of North Gnomerica Army during the Red Grass Revolution, and it grew around a river town beside his estate.  Families now visit Fort Gnomehagen's living-history grounds, the Confetti Reunion field, and the wonderfully solemn Museum of the World's Largest Piece of Confetti.  Gnome teachers insist the exhibit has deep cultural significance; the pupils mostly want to know whether it will fit through the door.
- **Major Facts:** Late fashion model Anna Gnicole attended school in the original river town, which also hosts the annual Gen G'ers celebration.  The Gnomerica State Supported Living Center began as a prisoner-of-war compound during Field Marshal Candy Corn's Circus War with a faction from Fairy Fen.  Today it is a place of care and community.  The Confetti Reunion celebrates the families who made peace afterward.  River's Belt is the main waterway, and its pilots still insist an empty boat must carry one lucky decorative brick.
- **Flora:** River reeds, apple blossoms
- **Fauna:** Tickhop, Potamice, Bloomlet
- **Bounties:**

### Gnafrica

- **Name:** Gnafrica
- **Location:** Southwest peninsula of Gnome Island, beyond the dry ridge.
- **Region:** Gnomadica, sunlands region.
- **About:** Golden grasslands give way to windy dunes and warm red rocks, so travelers bring plenty of water and very patient hats.  The desert tracks are kept by gnome well-keepers, lamia caravan guides, and giants who can spot an oasis from miles away.  Sockarillo outfits travelers for the heat, while Flybar tests gliders in the hot wind.  Every village owns a communal shade cloth, and arguments about who folded it last can outlast a sandstorm.
- **Major Facts:** The region holds the Windy Sands, two famous deserts, and two volcanoes said to have been named by rival surveyors who hated each other's poetry.  The Blue Stone Wellkeepers protect the safe watering places between the Windy Sands and the Desert of Desire, and every guide teaches visitors to spot a safe well by its blue-painted stones.  A traveling Magmander handler named Auntie Ember shows visitors how to cross volcanic ground without scorching their socks.  Guides from Grit Spit and Fellers still swear a third volcano exists, although their map marks it as 'probably lunch.'
- **Flora:** Sunneedle cactus, dune grass
- **Fauna:** Zapfox, Magmander, Pebblem
- **Bounties:**

### Gnufoundland

- **Name:** Gnufoundland
- **Location:** Cold island north of Gnome Island across the narrow sea.
- **Region:** Gnomadica, northern island region.
- **About:** Fog, cliffs, and long winters shape the northern communities, and every neighbor knows who borrowed the last warm scarf.  Weather comes in layers here: fog below, snow above, and gossip in the middle.  The docks of Gnyannis and Werewolf London are staffed by hearty fisherfolk, woolly-coated werewolves, and ogres who carry cargo as casually as a gnome carries a teacup.  New arrivals are offered soup before a shipping tariff.
- **Major Facts:** The Frozen Fields cover much of the interior.  When the outer sea freezes, winter shipping travels by sheltered coves, and the bay at Gnyannis becomes a refuge for ferries.  The Long Lantern Pact requires every coastal town to keep a lamp lit for travelers during winter storms, and werewolf families coordinate the night watch.  A giant knitted mitten hangs in the council chamber to honor the rescue of twelve sailors from a drifting ice floe.  Nobody knows which hand it was meant for.
- **Flora:** Salt pine, frost moss
- **Fauna:** Fluffbat, Hootgear, Glowbat
- **Bounties:**

### Slaanilok

- **Name:** Slaanilok
- **Location:** Eastern offshore island chain connected by ferries.
- **Region:** Gnomadica, eastern archipelago region.
- **About:** Boats are more common than carts in these cheerful island communities, and the ferries carry travelers, crates, and the occasional bewildered turtle.  Each island has its own ferry whistle and its own favorite explanation for why the sea is wet.  Lizard folk, lamia tide-readers, merfolk chandlers, gnomish net-menders, and cheerful turtle keepers bustle around warm harbors.  Visitors discover that the last boat of the day leaves precisely when the captain finishes supper.
- **Major Facts:** The Lantern Chain links its harbors to Port Gnasher by a line of signal lights, and the largest ports trade news the same way.  The first lantern keeper was a mermaid who wrote warnings on waterproof paper and charged by the vowel.  Ferries from Legipt carry the warm-season trade south to Eversummer, while older sea roads pass the flooded chambers of Dungeon of the Deep.  Islanders quietly disagree over whether the ancient bells beneath the water are musical instruments or doorbells, and all of them agree a dry pair of socks is the height of luxury.
- **Flora:** Saltgrass, mangroves
- **Fauna:** Jellybub, Shroomtle, Bloomlet
- **Bounties:**

### Slanic

- **Name:** Slanic
- **Location:** Southern river delta and marsh coast of Gnome Island.
- **Region:** Gnomadica, southern wetland region.
- **About:** Reed villages on stilts sit above winding waterways where little lights blink after dark.  Most locals know the difference between friendly bubbles and running-away bubbles, and most can tell the hour by the color of the mist.  The markets sell waterproof papers, glowcap lanterns, and shoes designed to be retrieved by string.  Fairies from Fairy Fen share the waterways with frog-keepers, friendly slimes, and lamia botanists who can carry six baskets without needing a cart.
- **Major Facts:** Fairy Fen and the Bog of Eternal Regret are here.  Ink-makers collect the dark pigment that Gloob leaves on the reeds, without disturbing the little slimes, and sell their finest bottles through Gloopenstein.  A centuries-old Reed Accord protects the fairy paths through Fairy Fen, including those of families who opposed Field Marshal Candy Corn's Circus War.  The flood council measures high water with a boot nailed to a post and takes it very seriously, although somebody keeps replacing the measuring boot with a larger one.  Mycelia's mushroom growers supply the region's famous midnight soup.
- **Flora:** Glowcap mushrooms, bog reeds
- **Fauna:** Jellybub, Shroomtle, Gloob, Goopamice
- **Bounties:**

### Gno Dao Cradle

- **Name:** Gno Dao Cradle
- **Location:** Northeast highlands of Gnome Island, above the old mountain road.
- **Region:** Gnomadica, eastern mountain region.
- **About:** Stone bridges, steep passes, and mountain workshops hanging from rock ledges fill the highlands, and the sound of hammering travels farther than conversation.  Even the goats wear bells so their owners can hear the complaints.  Goatfolk carry mail across the steepest paths; dwarven gemcutters, lamia archivists, and gnome mechanics work shoulder to shoulder beneath the mountain bells.  Every traveler earns a little brass pin after surviving the climb.
- **Major Facts:** The Gnomish Heights shelter the Cradle.  Cogbend Hold guards the lower pass, while Twinshift keeps the freight lifts moving in two daily shifts.  A chain of ancient stone markers points toward the 6th Age Ruins, the Ancient Device, and a fourth place no surveyor has managed to find.  The Fortresses of Easy Defeat, Moderation, and Impossibility were once a single training route.  Local historians are forbidden to declare any mysterious machinery 'the first ever' without checking the basement first.
- **Flora:** Ironfern, crystal lichen
- **Fauna:** Pebblem, Fluffbat, Cogsnake

## II. Cities, towns, and ports

### Cogbottom

- **Name:** Cogbottom
- **Location:** Center of Gnome Island, where three roads meet.
- **Region:** Gnomerica.
- **Factory Imports:** Socks; Banana; Clipboard
- **Factory Exports:** None
- **Non-Factory Imports:** coal
- **Non-Factory Exports:** sealed machinery crates; packing labels
- **About:** This noisy town is home to the Gnomeworks factory and its endlessly turning gears.  The machine makes useful tools, curious junk, and the occasional living Tiny Monster, whether anyone ordered them or not, which keeps the sorting crew very busy.  Brass carts rattle from the factory to Pulley Junction, and every neighbor knows the particular whistle that means a shipment of underpants has escaped quality control.  Shopkeepers price everything in both cogs and patience.
- **Major Facts:** The Packing Line ships from here.  Town inspectors once tried to catalog every strange item and ran out of forms before lunchtime.  The town's oldest argument is whether the giant machine is older than the building around it.  Its gear-keepers keep a ceremonial broom for sweeping runaway springs out of the streets.  On Founder's Crank Day, every household turns a small wooden cog to wish the factory another year of good fortune.  The council keeps a Lost and Found for orphaned socks, abandoned hats, and an alarming number of identical clipboards.
- **Flora:** Brassleaf oak, gearvine
- **Fauna:** Hootgear, Cogsnake, Pebblem
- **Bounties:**

### Gnew York

- **Name:** Gnew York
- **Location:** East-central main island at the crossroads of the coast road.
- **Region:** Gnomerica.
- **Factory Imports:** Printer; Necktie; Wastebasket
- **Factory Exports:** None
- **Non-Factory Imports:** street lamps
- **Non-Factory Exports:** posters; gossip broadsheets
- **About:** Tall clock towers and crowded streets give this city a proud, hurried attitude, and everyone walks fast, even to the bakery next door.  Stacked bakeries, brass elevators, and rooftop gardens rise between the towers, and the gardeners argue over the correct height for a tomato.  A lamia tram conductor named Miss Coil keeps the morning commuters moving and can check six tickets before the light changes.  Street musicians play the city's famous tune, 'Honk If You Have Brakes.'
- **Major Facts:** The mayor holds press conferences from a crate.  The city built its first elevated tram after the mayor mistook a traffic jam for a parade.  Pigeons gather beneath the old mayoral balcony, where a pigeon named Colonel Crumb has been elected unofficial traffic marshal three times and rules the curb with an iron beak.  Port Gnasher carries the eastbound freight that Gnew York cannot fit on its trains.  Every autumn the city stages the Great Hat Migration, a festival where residents swap their least sensible headwear.
- **Flora:** Brassleaf oak, gearvine
- **Fauna:** Hootgear, Cogsnake, Pebblem

### Port Gnaru

- **Name:** Port Gnaru
- **Location:** Western coast at the mouth of River's Belt.
- **Region:** Gnohio.
- **Factory Imports:** Hook; Toolbox; Toilet Paper
- **Factory Exports:** None
- **Non-Factory Imports:** harbor chains
- **Non-Factory Exports:** river fish; ship manifests
- **About:** The western docks are always muddy, busy, and one crate away from chaos.  Here the river barges of Gnohio meet the open sea under a great brass crane nicknamed Old Shoulder.  Stevedores include gnomes, riverfolk, and a giant named Mabel who loads ships by hand but refuses to handle fragile teapots.  The fish stalls claim the tide has an appetite of its own.
- **Major Facts:** The harbor bell rings when the tide turns.  A retired crab keeper trained several actual crabs to ring it, and some days the crabs hold the town schedule hostage.  Warehouses receive wheat from Farms of Verdon't, timber from the western woods, and strange machine goods bound for Slaanilok.  The Customs Office keeps a drawer of suspiciously festive paperwork to compare against real dock seals.  Nobody admits why that drawer is jingling.
- **Flora:** River reeds, saltgrass
- **Fauna:** Potamice, Bloomlet

### Port Gnasher

- **Name:** Port Gnasher
- **Location:** Eastern tip of the main island, facing Slaanilok.
- **Region:** Gnomerica.
- **Factory Imports:** Saw; Fishing Rod; Fire Extinguisher
- **Factory Exports:** None
- **Non-Factory Imports:** dock timber
- **Non-Factory Exports:** ferry tickets; salted kelp
- **About:** The port's name sounds fierce, but its busiest workers are cheerful dock gnomes.  Harbor boats paint smiling teeth on their bows because the old reef looks like the jaws of an enormous sea beast.  Most crews will happily give directions, though they may charge a biscuit for the favor.  A lamia harbor pilot named Captain Nera knows every safe channel and every sailor's favorite complaint.
- **Major Facts:** Port Gnasher runs the eastern ferry service.  Captain Nera's grandmother mapped the shoals before the signal lanterns were built, earning a statue that the gulls treat with disrespect.  The ferry captains trade lamps with Slaanilok so a lost traveler can always find a welcoming coast.  The lighthouse keeper's ledger notes a few suspicious sleigh-bell sounds from boats arriving at night, but the town refuses to accuse every festive visitor of being a smuggler.
- **Flora:** Sea lavender, saltgrass
- **Fauna:** Jellybub, Shroomtle
- **Bounties:**

### Gloopenstein

- **Name:** Gloopenstein
- **Location:** Southern hill above the marsh road.
- **Region:** Slanic.
- **Factory Imports:** Soap; Bucket; Test Tube
- **Factory Exports:** None
- **Non-Factory Imports:** water pumps
- **Non-Factory Exports:** glow dye; slime-safe glass
- **About:** Green windows gleam across this tidy, slightly squishy city of glass tubes, warm fountains, and canals with the consistency of soup.  The streets are swept twice a day and somehow stay sticky.  Its schools teach slime husbandry alongside mathematics, and nobody laughs when a Jellybub needs a little extra time to cross the road.  Inventors arrive hoping to discover an amazing new glue; locals beg them to invent a better mop.
- **Major Facts:** The municipal slime clock loses time whenever it rains, because the pendulum keeps stopping to look at puddles.  The town academy has called this 'promising research' for thirty years.  The Academy of Respectable Goo studies the natural pigments left by Gloob, while Slanic's marsh farmers harvest the reeds used for ink.  A lamia apothecary named Doctor Liss runs the city's most dependable clinic and reminds visitors that slimes need clean water as much as anyone else.
- **Flora:** Glowcap mushrooms, bog mint
- **Fauna:** Jellybub, Gloob
- **Bounties:**

### Sockarillo

- **Name:** Sockarillo
- **Location:** Southwest desert highway beside a cool spring.
- **Region:** Gnafrica.
- **Factory Imports:** Socks; Work Boot; Bathtub
- **Factory Exports:** None
- **Non-Factory Imports:** water barrels
- **Non-Factory Exports:** desert wool; pepper dust
- **About:** Sockarillo trades boots and blankets to travelers crossing the hot lands.  Travelers know it by its enormous sock sign, visible for miles, the smell of hot flatbread, and shop windows full of hats built to resist sandstorms.  Lamia merchants run shaded caravan stalls, while goblin cobblers argue over whether a boot should have a left and a right side.  The town's drinking fountain is treated with the respect usually reserved for kings.
- **Major Facts:** The Annual Sock Parade shuts down Main Street.  It began when a merchant mistook the governor's laundry line for festival bunting.  The winning sock must stand upright without a frame or invisible wire, and every year someone enters a boot and claims it is a sock with ambitions.  A veteran guide named Old Sandie keeps a public well registry, copied from the Blue Stone Wellkeepers.
- **Flora:** Sunneedle cactus, desert sage
- **Fauna:** Zapfox, Magmander
- **Bounties:**

### Cogington

- **Name:** Cogington
- **Location:** Northwest of Cogbottom on the brass road.
- **Region:** Gnomerica.
- **Factory Imports:** Pen; Clipboard; Necktie
- **Factory Exports:** None
- **Non-Factory Imports:** paving stones
- **Non-Factory Exports:** business permits; clock fittings
- **About:** A proper town of tidy workshops, polished front steps, and very serious small hats, proud of keeping the gears on time.  Market traders measure spring tension with pocket gauges, and councilors wear hats proportionate to their opinions.  Visitors can shop for brass fittings before crossing the square to Dice and Doodles for an afternoon of drawing and tabletop adventures.  The local owls seem especially fond of paper scraps.
- **Major Facts:** The town council has seventeen committees and only twelve chairs, so meetings often begin with a polite race to sit down and never start on time.  The town sponsors an annual Student Invention Fair with Cogsworth; one winning entry was a kettle that applauded itself.  The Council Archivist, a retired ogre named Penny, keeps the only complete list of which inventions actually worked.  She refuses to count any object that catches fire during its demonstration.
- **Flora:** Brassleaf oak, gearvine
- **Fauna:** Hootgear, Cogsnake, Pebblem

### Cogsworth

- **Name:** Cogsworth
- **Location:** East of Cogbottom along the workshop rail.
- **Region:** Gnomerica.
- **Factory Imports:** Spare Cog; Magnet; Alarm Clock
- **Factory Exports:** None
- **Non-Factory Imports:** steel plate
- **Non-Factory Exports:** precision springs; repair manuals
- **About:** Cogsworth builds tiny parts with enormous care.  It is a town of fine gears and watch springs, where mechanics listen to a clock the way musicians listen to an orchestra and can hear a loose bolt from across the square.  Lamia machinists run narrow lathes with extraordinary precision, and the town baker has invented a gear-shaped pastry with far too many teeth.  Cogsworth's freight travels to Cogbottom along the central rail.
- **Major Facts:** The clock tower chimes thirteen at noon.  Its builder, Master Gnoseph Cogsworth, refused to waste a perfectly good bell and maintained that silence was an engineering error.  Every spring, apprentices march to Pulley Junction to compare clocks with the railway timekeeper, then spend three days arguing about which clock cheated.  Newly hired mechanics must fix one broken toy for a child before working on public machinery.
- **Flora:** Brassleaf oak, gearvine
- **Fauna:** Hootgear, Cogsnake, Pebblem
- **Bounties:**

### Sproing

- **Name:** Sproing
- **Location:** North-central main island near the foothills.
- **Region:** Gnomerica.
- **Factory Imports:** Clamp; Ladder; Balloon
- **Factory Exports:** None
- **Non-Factory Imports:** spring steel
- **Non-Factory Exports:** coiled springs; spring-mounted handcarts
- **About:** In Sproing, even the benches have suspiciously lively cushions.  The town nestles among soft hills where every fence seems to hold a spring, every garden gate bounces, and even the goats walk with suspicious enthusiasm.  Engineers test cushions, shock absorbers, and peculiar pogo carts here before shipping them to Cogbottom.  Visitors are handed a safety helmet and a warning about ceiling fans.
- **Major Facts:** The town is proud of its accidental inventions, and the Great Bounce Festival celebrates the most famous one, the self-returning park bench.  The mayor once gave a speech from a trampoline and had to be rescued from a nearby roof by a kindly giant.  Trampoline speeches are now banned, and a brass statue of the giant stands outside the hall holding a tiny umbrella.  The school teaches children to check the landing area before admiring how high anything flies.
- **Flora:** Brassleaf oak, gearvine
- **Fauna:** Hootgear, Cogsnake, Pebblem

### Flybar

- **Name:** Flybar
- **Location:** Southwest cliffs above the coastal winds.
- **Region:** Gnafrica.
- **Factory Imports:** Kite; Scarf; Battery
- **Factory Exports:** None
- **Non-Factory Imports:** canvas wings
- **Non-Factory Exports:** gliders; wind charts
- **About:** Rope bridges, windmills, canvas gliders, and little flying machines fill this breezy cliff town.  Visitors are told to hold onto their hats and their lunches, and kitchens weigh down their plates before opening the windows.  Its pilots come from all kinds of families, including a fairy engineer whose wing designs are much safer than the name 'Almost Certainly Fine' suggests.  Below the cliffs, the Windy Sands ripple toward Sockarillo.
- **Major Facts:** Pilots use the ridge to test new wings, and they hold a friendly contest with Sproing to see whose springs can launch the safest glider.  The oldest windsock has been promoted to honorary flight captain, outranking several pilots who crashed into the same haystack.  A giant sailmaker stitches emergency landing nets for the launch platform.  Pilots who forget to buy a return ticket are expected to walk home.
- **Flora:** Sunneedle cactus, dune grass
- **Fauna:** Zapfox, Magmander, Pebblem

### Pulley Junction

- **Name:** Pulley Junction
- **Location:** Central rail crossing just south of Cogbottom.
- **Region:** Gnomerica.
- **Factory Imports:** Wrench; Nut and Bolt; Cardboard Box
- **Factory Exports:** None
- **Non-Factory Imports:** rail ties
- **Non-Factory Exports:** rail coupling pins; lifting rigs
- **About:** Tracks, cranes, and carts weave together at this busy transfer town.  Cranes hang over switching yards where Cogbottom's crates join river cargo heading west and mountain ore heading northeast.  Lamia dispatchers use color-coded rail cards, while troll bridge crews keep the tracks safe when a wagon weighs more than its paperwork.  If a shipment goes missing, it usually turns up under a bigger shipment.
- **Major Facts:** The station clock runs five minutes fast because a former manager believed punctuality could be manufactured.  No one can agree which five minutes it stole.  Freight from Twinshift passes through on its way to Cogbottom and Port Gnaru, while goods for the island ferries continue toward Gnew York.  The famous Missing Crate Office has found every lost box except the one holding its own original door sign.
- **Flora:** Brassleaf oak, gearvine
- **Fauna:** Hootgear, Cogsnake, Pebblem

### Brassin

- **Name:** Brassin
- **Location:** Southeast coast of the main island, near the marsh outlet.
- **Region:** Slanic.
- **Factory Imports:** Soap; Bucket; Frying Pan
- **Factory Exports:** None
- **Non-Factory Imports:** copper ore
- **Non-Factory Exports:** polished brass; boat bells
- **About:** Brassin shines even on foggy days.  Copper domes and polished bells line its workshop streets, reflected in the muddy water of Slanic's canals, and its bells can be heard across the reeds.  Lamia bellcasters, gnome smiths, and fairy lantern-makers share the open foundry square.  The whole place smells pleasantly of hot metal and marsh mint.
- **Major Facts:** Every town bell has a name, and visitors learn that the nickname is often more accurate than the official label.  The biggest, Please Stop That, was cast to warn river pilots of fog and now rings whenever someone drops a hammer.  Brassin's apprentices trade parts with Gnome'n'Pinion and practice safe sharpening at Don't Grind Angry.  The town honors the Reed Accord by keeping the old fairy footbridge, even after building a much grander steel one beside it.
- **Flora:** Glowcap mushrooms, bog reeds

### Brasstop

- **Name:** Brasstop
- **Location:** Northern ridge above Cogsworth.
- **Region:** Gnomerica.
- **Factory Imports:** Toolbox; Lightbulb; Gloves
- **Factory Exports:** None
- **Non-Factory Imports:** tin ore
- **Non-Factory Exports:** brass plaques; roof vents
- **About:** A rooftop town high on a ridge above Cogsworth, built around a hill of chimneys, shiny weather vanes, and brass roofs that glitter at dawn.  Its residents insist more polish fixes most problems.  A family of giant roofers does the dangerous repairs, with a gnome clerk below calling measurements through a speaking tube. - **Location:** The island west of Gnafrica's coast, with a long narrow neck to the north and a wide body to the south.
- **Region:** Gnomadica, its own region.
- **About:** A moon-named island with a long rocky neck, a deep bay on its northeast side, woods and hills in its middle, and three old standing stones on its southern hills.  On clear nights the moon looks bigger here than anywhere else.
- **Major Facts:** Zohee Bay, the Three Idols, and Demon's Gutt are here, plus a small town on Zohee Bay.

### Three Idols

- **Name:** Three Idols
- **Location:** Southern hills of Gnonmoon.
- **Region:** Gnonmoon.
- **About:** Three tall standing stones on a hilltop, side by side like singers on a stage.  The middle stone is the tallest, and on windy nights the hilltop hums.
- **Major Facts:** Nobody remembers who raised the stones, or why they hum.

### Tables Edge

- **Name:** Tables Edge
- **Location:** Deep in the pine forest in the middle of Shen Shin.
- **Region:** Shen Shin.
- **Factory Imports:** Sneaker; Work Boot; Ruler; Triangle Ruler; Hammer; Saw
- **Factory Exports:** Log; Chair; Boomerang; Cardboard Box
- **Non-Factory Imports:** None
- **Non-Factory Exports:** tables; pine benches; spare table legs; sawdust for packing crates
- **About:** A woodworking town hidden deep in the pines, where every house has a workshop and every workshop makes tables.  Big tables, small tables, tables for giants and tables for gnomes.  Watch out for your shins.
- **Major Facts:** Shen Shin's busiest town, where most of the island's shipping goes.  Nobody has counted how many shins its table corners have claimed.
