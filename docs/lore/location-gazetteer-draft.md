# Gnomeworks: Location Gazetteer (Working Draft)

**Prepared for:** Nathan and Alex  
**Draft date:** November 3, 2026  
**Scope:** Gnome Island and its surrounding regions in Gnomadica.  Exactly **100 locations**, grouped by category.  No classification, geography, imports, exports, or local stories are approved canon until reviewed.

## How to read this draft

- **Location** means a *proposed* place on the one master map, described in relative directions so it can be moved when the map is drawn.  There are no fixed coordinates yet.
- **Region** gives the proposed parent area.  Gnomadica covers the mapped island and nearby offshore areas, not the whole planet.  The North Pole and wider world remain outside the map.
- **[Factory]** identifies an *exact existing collectible name* from Gnomeworks' 148-item catalog in `docs/INVENTORY.md` and `js/config.js`.  It does **not** mean the item is exclusive to the factory or that a shipping order is implemented.
- **[World]** means an **additional proposed trade good** for fiction or future mechanics.  It is **not currently a factory collectible**.  World goods can appear as imports or exports, including services and information.
- This is a place registry for future shipping, lore cards, and expeditions.  A place can serve several purposes even though it is filed under one primary category here.  Some entries may remain hidden from players until discovered.
- **Bounties** is intentionally empty in every entry for future use.  These are story hooks, not implemented quests or promised rewards.
- The factory company name, machine origin, and regional histories remain open.  This file is a working draft published on GitHub for Nathan and Alex to review.  Publication does not establish canon.
