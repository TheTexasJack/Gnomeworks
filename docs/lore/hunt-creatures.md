```text
# Reference Image: Cockatrice
Name: Noluu Bird
Description: -- This creature appears to fly at a speed of 400 km/h without using its wings at all! It also seems to have a venom it uses to strike at its
prey that turns the prey to stone. Honestly its not looking at it that turns you to stone I do not know who started that rumor. - Dr. QuartzHawk Dragon

# Boar
Mr. Fluffy (boss)(Not Implemented)
-- Somebodies pet from Smallerville escaped a few years ago and turned into this beast. I would love to get more information from this thing
but whenever I get near it I.. "LOVE ME, PET ME, RUB MY BELLY". Apologies even writing about the effects triggers it. It is not friendly in the least, suffice to say. - Dr. Quartz

Multibutted Boar
-- As a result of the locals requesting boar butts from adventurers, the boars of the area have adapted by growing butts similar in way
to a hydra. Cut off one butt and two butts shall take its place, so on and so forth. I was unsure which butt relieves waste when I witnessed 
an elderly specimen that had 40 butts. I guess some questions should never have answers. - Dr. Quartz

# Panther with Tenticles
The Hizzerl sic
-- Local legends from I Crant Spellit sic tell of a feline like entity that sucks dry the fluids of any who sweat near it. No I am not
joking, sweat. I believe it needs a higher amount of salt than a normal creature. Despite what the gnomes of the area will tell you there
are a good number of those things there. - Dr. Quartz

# Legged Trout
Snarling Piscine
-- Found all over the rivers of Gnomatica. Minor psionic abilities but quite slow on land. I enjoy these things roasted over a campfire
with a squirt of lemon, just try to ignore the screams as you cook them. - Dr. Quartz

# Mushroom Turtle
Sporebeak
-- These beasts are quite lazy during winter in Mycelia as that is when I initially observed them. Big mistake visiting them during the summer as that is when Mycelia is covered in a giant spore cloud that the beasts generate. Get too close to the creatures and the whole thing goes up in flames! It turns out the Sporebeak breathes fire and the spores are highly flammable. Thank goodness Mycelia has a thriving clothing industry or I would be completely nude for the rest of my expedition! - Dr. Quartz now reclothed

# Hollaring Elk
Hoolopeater
-- "Beware the Hollopeater, my child!
    The jaws that bite, the hooves that trample!
    Beware the Noluu bird, and shun
    The frumious Thunderampal!" - an excerpt from the Hoolopeater poem

# Crystal Drake
Sinner of Smallerville
--Whispers tell of this drake that once upon a time it committed great sin in Smallerville. My personal studies show the only sinning it's capable of doing is shooting its crystalline quills in all directions. - Dr. Quartz

# Banana Monster
Kilar Nanana sic
-- First discovered in I Crant Spellit sic these creatures spawn from banana plantations and have been seen all over Gnomatica. I am unsure if they serve as guardians similar to treants or if they are simply born from the banana tree and cause chaos like the goblin. From afar, I have witnessed these beasts eating each other when their population was dense. According to hunters their insides are all banana. Sadly I can only get close-up information from hunters and they are only interested in eating them! - Dr. Quartz

# Hawk Dragon
Improbable Gryphon
-- Typically flying high above the Fortress of Impossibility this creature seems to possess abilities that would be, well improbable. Each
individual of the species seems to be unique with their abilities. One will have a scorpion tail, another will have 2 heads, yet another will
have psionic powers, I have witnessed one to be a hivemind. The only things these creatures have in common is a general shape, that of the
humble gryphon. I have many a theory that these beasts simultaneously have yet to exist and have always existed such is their improbability.
Dr. Quartz may have eaten too many sweets with his gnog
```

## Notes for Nathan (Alex's words above stay as written)

- The map's name is Gnomadica; Alex writes "Gnomatica" twice.  Dr. Quartz could just spell it wrong, which suits him.
- The elk's name appears as both Hoolopeater and Hollopeater.
- Mr. Fluffy is a new boss idea.  It is parked until the hunt gets another pass, behind the Packing Line.
- The in-game creatures still show their old names.  Renaming them to Alex's names is a small game change for later.
- The poem is a send-up of Lewis Carroll's "Jabberwocky" (1871, public domain), so it is safe to use.
